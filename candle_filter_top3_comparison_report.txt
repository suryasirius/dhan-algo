Candle-Analysis Confirmation Filter Applied to the Top 3 Strategies -- 6-Month Comparison
============================================================================================

WHAT WAS DONE
---------------
Took your candle-analysis engine (the 9-concept Price Action & Market Structure compendium,
run UNRESTRICTED this time -- all 9 concepts active, no Tue/Wed-only filter, no HTF trend
filter, confluence=1, so it's being used purely as a general-purpose confirmation signal, not
its already-narrowed live configuration) and used it as an EXTRA CONDITION on top of each of
the top 3 strategies from your list: a trade from each strategy is only kept if the candle
engine also fired an agreeing signal (final_sell for SHORT/PE trades, final_buy for LONG/CE
trades) within the 10 minutes before or at that trade's own entry.

Data: same 6-month local Nifty dataset used throughout (2026-02-02 to 2026-07-31, 122 trading
days), resampled to 2-minute bars to match how Velez/Crash Play/Opening-Drive were originally
built and tested. Each strategy kept its own money-model convention (Velez = raw index points
x lot size, no delta; Crash Play & Opening-Drive = delta-scaled option-premium proxy) and its
own stop/trail setting (8pt stop / 4pt trail, each strategy's established default) so the
baseline-vs-filtered comparison is apples-to-apples within each strategy.

READING THIS TABLE
---------------------
1. VELEZ 8PT: filtering cut trade count from 63 to 23 (63% fewer trades). Win rate stayed
   basically flat (34.9% -> 34.8%), but profit factor jumped to 3.47. FLAG: PF > 2.5 is this
   project's own established red-flag threshold -- with only 23 trades, this needs more data
   before trusting it; it's very likely a small-sample effect (the filter happened to remove
   some of the biggest losers) rather than a proven improvement. Net Rs and max drawdown both
   fell in proportion to the trade-count drop, so filtering didn't clearly IMPROVE money made
   per unit of risk here -- it mostly just reduced volume.

2. CRASH PLAY: this is the most encouraging result. Baseline was a net LOSER at this stop/trail
   setting (PF 0.92, net -Rs 1,079) -- consistent with this project's own notes that Crash Play
   needed a wider sweep to find its working combo, and 8/4 wasn't necessarily it. Once filtered
   by the candle-analysis signal, it flipped to a small net winner (PF 1.46, net +Rs 2,370) on
   19 trades. Still a very small sample and still a modest edge, but the DIRECTION of change
   (loser -> winner) is a real, not just cosmetic, effect.

3. OPENING-DRIVE MOMENTUM: zero trades survived the filter. This isn't a bug -- Opening-Drive
   Momentum's whole design is to fire fast, in the first 30 minutes after market open (09:15-
   09:45). The candle-analysis engine's own concepts need 14-30 bars of warmup (ATR, ADX, the
   weak-arc rollover check) before they can vote at all, which eats up most or all of that same
   09:15-09:45 window. The two signals structurally don't have time to overlap on the same day
   -- this is a timing mismatch, not evidence the candle analysis disagrees with Opening-Drive's
   trades.

CAVEATS
---------
- Every filtered sample here is small (19-23 trades over 6 months). None of these numbers
  should be treated as a settled verdict -- they're a first look, not a validated edge.
- Crash Play's baseline used the same 8pt/4pt setting as Velez and Opening-Drive for
  consistency, but Crash Play's own prior sweep in this project didn't land on one single
  "locked" default the way the other two did -- so this baseline may understate what Crash Play
  can normally do on its own best combo.
- No in-sample/out-of-sample split was run on these filtered subsets -- with 19-23 trades,
  splitting in half would leave ~10 trades per half, too thin to be meaningful.

VERDICT
--------
Worth a longer look, not yet a strategy change:
- Crash Play + candle-filter is the standout -- turned a loser into a winner and is worth
  testing over a longer window and with Crash Play's own better-tuned stop/trail combo (not
  just the 8/4 default borrowed from the other two).
- Velez + candle-filter needs more trades before the PF 3.47 number can be trusted; treat it
  as "needs verification," not "confirmed better."
- Opening-Drive + candle-filter isn't a workable combination as designed -- the two signals
  operate in different, non-overlapping parts of the trading day.
