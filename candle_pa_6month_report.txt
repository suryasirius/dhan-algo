STRATEGY AS TESTED (matches your already-locked dhan_live_bot.py configuration)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Only concepts 2 (Compression Spike, sell) and 4 (Rollover, sell) are active. The other seven
  (1,3,5,6,7,8,9) are the ones your own prior testing in this project found unprofitable or
  too thin on data, so they stay switched off here too -- this is not a new decision, just
  carrying forward what you'd already established.
- Filters: Tuesday/Wednesday only (Thu/Fri were net losers in your prior 6-month test); the
  5-minute AND 15-minute EMA(9/21) trend must both agree with the signal direction.
- Exits: fixed 5-point stop-loss / 10-point target (1:2 risk:reward), no trailing -- this is
  the exact "previous" stop-loss and target you asked me to reuse. A trailing-stop version was
  tried before and dropped (underperformed on 1-min close-only data), so it wasn't retested here.
- Max holding time: 15 bars, then a time exit.
- Money simulation (proxy, since Dhan doesn't expose historical option premiums -- documented,
  known platform limitation, not re-investigated here): Nifty lot size 65, assumed delta 0.6,
  1 lot/trade, Rs 40 brokerage per round trip, Rs 10,000 starting capital.

RIGOR FIX APPLIED
------------------
The original backtest.py filled a new position at the SAME bar's close that generated the
signal -- a look-ahead fill. This run fixes that: entries fill at the OPEN of the next bar.
The effect was small (86 trades / Rs 9,644 net with the fix vs 85 trades / Rs 9,932 without),
so the original result wasn't meaningfully inflated by this bug, but the fixed number is the
one to trust.

STANDARD SL x TARGET SWEEP (5 stop-loss values x 6 target values, 30 combos; full grid in
candle_pa_6month_sweep.csv)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Best by SQN: SL=4 / TP=12 -> PF 2.22, SQN 2.84, 85 trades, net P&L (proxy) Rs 12,916,
max drawdown (proxy) Rs 1,827.
Runner-up combos (SL=10/TP=12, SL=6/TP=12, SL=12/TP=12) all cluster around SQN ~2.5-2.6,
suggesting TP=12 is a genuine local sweet spot rather than a fluke of one combo.

Grid-edge check: SL=4 sits at this grid's low boundary, so the grid was extended down to
SL=2 and SL=3 (with TP re-checked at 8/10/12/16) to see if performance kept climbing toward
an even tighter stop. It didn't -- SL=3/TP=12 (SQN 2.80) and SL=2/TP=12 (SQN 2.79) both come
in slightly BELOW SL=4/TP=12 (SQN 2.84), i.e. results level off rather than keep climbing.
That's the signature of a real interior optimum, not the "picking up pennies in front of a
steamroller" tail-risk-harvesting pattern flagged elsewhere in this project. SL=4/TP=12 is a
reasonable candidate to trade; it is tighter than your original 5/10 and worth considering as
an update, but 5/10 remains defensible too (PF/SQN both still solid, and it's the parameter
set already live-validated).
