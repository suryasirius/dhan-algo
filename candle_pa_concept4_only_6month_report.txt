WHY I'M NOT CALLING THIS A CLEAN WIN YET
--------------------------------------------
Two things temper how much to trust the 62.5%-win-rate, PF-4.55 number:

1. Profit factor 4.55 is well above this project's own red-flag line (PF > 2.5 has meant a
   bug or artifact every other time it's shown up here) -- so this needs to be treated as
   "needs verification," not as a proven edge.

2. Only 16 trades occurred in 6 months (about 1 every 11 days). That's too few to draw a
   confident conclusion either way -- one single trade (2026-06-23, +79.85 points, nearly 8x
   the intended 10-point target) accounts for a large share of the total profit. Remove that
   one trade and the picture looks much more ordinary.

3. Both this run and the full test use close-only stop/target checking on 1-min candles (an
   established convention in this project) -- which means a trade can overshoot its 5-point
   stop or 10-point target by a wide margin if price moves fast between candle closes. That's
   exactly what happened on the outlier trade above. It's not a bug, but it does mean the
   real-world result would likely be smaller (a broker would have gotten you out closer to
   the actual 5/10 levels via a live stop order, not let it run to +79.85).

An in-sample/out-of-sample split isn't meaningful here -- 16 trades split in half is only
8 trades per half, far too small to say anything reliable.

VERDICT
--------
Directionally interesting (Concept 4 alone does look like the stronger of your two active
concepts), but NOT yet something to trade in isolation with confidence. Before switching to
"Concept 4 only": (a) let more months of data accumulate so the sample grows past 16 trades,
and (b) consider re-testing with a live/simulated stop order (not close-only) so the outlier
overshoot doesn't inflate the number. For now, running the combined 2+4 strategy (which
dilutes Concept 4's higher win rate but adds 4x the trade volume) remains the safer, better-
supported choice.
